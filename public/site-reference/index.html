Taxonomy

Content Taxonomy: Process vs Services vs Packages

These three concepts are distinct and should never be conflated in navigation labels, page headings, or copy.

Concept What It Is Lives At Items
Process The client journey. Sequential phases every engagement goes through. Not a product. Not purchasable. How it works. /process/ Consult → Design → Install → Maintain
Services The disciplines GOE practices and the a la carte offerings available to existing clients. Educational and capability-focused. What GOE knows how to do. /ecological-landscaping-services/ and sub-pages Design & Consultation, Installation, Maintenance, Commercial. Plus a la carte: mulching, hardscaping, seasonal care, gardening services.
Packages The scope tiers. How much land, how deep, how comprehensive. These have defined deliverables and price ranges. The conversion-focused offering. What you buy. /design-packages/ The Kitchen Garden, The Food Forest, The Estate Plan, The Commercial Build
